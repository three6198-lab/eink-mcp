About
Works with e-paper displays running EPD-nRF5 firmware on nRF51822 / nRF52810 / nRF52811 MCUs. Common sources include repurposed retail electronic shelf labels (ESL).

Compatibility: Android (Chrome 56+), macOS/Windows (Chrome/Edge), iOS (requires Bluefy browser).
